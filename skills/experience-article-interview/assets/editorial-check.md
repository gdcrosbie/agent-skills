# Editorial check

Optional working record. Keep it outside the clean article and focus on substantive
findings rather than displaying a completed checklist by default.

## Argument and authorship

- The point the author is making:
- The specific experience or judgement that earns it:
- Any statement attributed to the author without supporting answers:
- The strongest objection and whether it is treated fairly:
- Assumptions, limits or counterexamples the reader needs:

## Structure and prose

- Does the opening make the next paragraph worth reading?
- Does each section advance the argument?
- Where should repetition, digressions or generic advice be cut?
- Does sentence rhythm feel natural, with connected prose and useful lists?
- Does the ending land the idea without becoming an advert?
- Are the profile's language variant, terminology, punctuation and description rules
  applied? Exclude literal code, URLs and quotations from punctuation changes.
- Remove hype and unsupported claims about experience.

## Evidence and readiness

- Claims checked, with sources recorded in the working notes:
- Personal details and example permissions confirmed by the author:
- Facts qualified or removed because evidence was unavailable:
- Material unresolved questions:
- Status: ready for the author's review / ready for publication / needs answers.

Use ready for publication only when material factual and authorship gaps are resolved.
This status does not authorise uploading or publishing the article.

## Handover

- Clean title, standfirst and complete article in Markdown.
- Research and review notes kept separate.
- Destination metadata included only when needed and checked against its current format.
- Optional social post: one useful idea, substance in the opening, confirmed URL or no
  link, plain-text paragraphs and a `.txt` file where supported.
- If preserving an approved version, compare the wording and paragraph breaks exactly.
